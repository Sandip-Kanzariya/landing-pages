PROCESS / 4 MIN READ

Living at home while the work is underway.

If you plan to stay in your home during a renovation, talk through access, noise, and daily routines before work begins.

Understand which rooms are affected

Ask when each area will be unavailable, whether there will be a temporary route through the work zone, and how the team will separate work areas from living spaces.

Plan for the essentials

If a kitchen or bathroom is involved, discuss what will remain usable and when. A simple temporary setup can make a major difference to daily routines.

Agree on a communication rhythm

Find out who to contact with a question, when you can expect updates, and how schedule changes will be shared. Knowing what is happening next helps everyone plan their day.

Be realistic about comfort

Noise, dust, deliveries, and changing access can make a home feel different during construction. Ask the team what controls and daily cleanup practices they expect to use, and decide together whether staying elsewhere for part of the project makes more sense.

Every project and household is different. Discuss site safety, access, and living arrangements directly with the qualified professionals managing your project.
