MATERIALS / 5 MIN READ

Choosing finishes you will be happy to live with.

The right material is not only the one that looks good in a sample. Think about how it will feel and function in your home.

Look at samples where they will live

Light changes a color through the day. Bring samples into the room and view them in morning light, evening light, and alongside the existing surfaces you plan to keep.

Compare care, not just color

Ask what regular cleaning looks like, whether a surface needs periodic sealing or refinishing, and how it responds to the everyday wear your household expects. A beautiful finish should fit your appetite for upkeep.

See the whole palette together

Collect the larger pieces—flooring, cabinet finish, tile, paint, hardware—in one place. A palette often feels different when the materials are viewed together rather than as separate selections.

Confirm availability and lead times

Before a choice is final, ask the project team to confirm the exact product, finish, quantity, and expected arrival. Substitutions can affect adjacent choices, so review them together.

This article is general project-planning information. Product performance and suitability depend on the material, installation, and use; confirm details with qualified suppliers and professionals.
