STUDIO DESK / ENQUIRIES

Begin with an object.

A useful brief can be short. Tell us what you make, where the images will be seen, and which detail tends to disappear in photographs.

01 / THE BRIEF

Three things to include.

  • A snapshot in daylight, with a familiar object alongside for scale.
  • The intended use: a shop listing, a printed catalog, or a launch story.
  • The date you need final artwork and whether packaging is ready.
