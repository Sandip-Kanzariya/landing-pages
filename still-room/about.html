STUDIO PROFILE / ABOUT

Look at what the material does.

Still Room is a concept art-direction studio for independent makers. Each study starts with one question: what would you notice if you could pick the object up?

METHOD / ON THE TABLE

One object, several readings.

For Serein, we paired a bottle with the carton it arrives in. The glass catches the light at its edges; the paper reveals its shape in shadow. Keeping the viewpoint similar makes the difference visible.

In the Low Table collection, the comparison is between a cup you hold and a dish that stays put. The rim, foot and handle tell you how each is meant to be used.
