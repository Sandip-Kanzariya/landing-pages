WHAT WE TEACH

Build your AI skills, one system at a time.

Focused learning paths help you design, evaluate, and deploy AI applications through practical, end-to-end projects.

PROGRAMS

Choose your build path.

01 / FOUNDATIONS

Applied LLMs

Build a language-model application that answers from your own documents. Learn retrieval, prompt design, and how to evaluate grounded answers.

02 / WORKFLOWS

Agent Systems

Design tool-using agents with clear state, checkpoints, and human approval. Explore how to coordinate multi-step tasks and handle failure.

03 / DEPLOYMENT

Production AI

Move an AI prototype toward a maintainable service. Package an API, deploy a container, and monitor latency and cost.

HOW IT WORKS

From brief to working demo.

Every path starts with a user problem and ends with a tested project. Work through the engineering choices step by step, then leave with a demo and notes you can use to explain your approach.
