Before you arrive.

Your first session starts with a quick introduction, not a fitness test. Here is what a visit would look like.

01 / Pick a time

Find your class

For a first lift, choose Strength lab. For a shorter session, try Saturday Groundwork. Check the weekly timetable for start times.

02 / Get settled

Come ten minutes early

Bring water, clean training shoes and clothes you can move in. The coach will show you where to leave your bag and introduce your first station.
