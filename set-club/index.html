01  /  The training

Three ways in.

Pick a focus for the day. Every class starts with a walkthrough, so nobody has to guess what comes next.

A / 50 min

Strength lab

Learn a lift, practice in pairs, and take your time between sets. Bodyweight options are part of the plan.

B / 40 min

Engine room

Short rounds on ropes, bikes, and rowers. Work at your own pace; the whole group rests together.

C / 30 min

Groundwork

Trade the clock for a mat. Slow core work and mobility drills give you room to focus on control.

03  /  First visit

New here?
Good.

You don’t need to get fit before joining a gym. Your first session is for finding your footing.

Which class should I try?

Start with Strength lab if you want to learn the basics of a lift, or choose Groundwork for a shorter mat-based session.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes, clean training shoes, and a water bottle. Mats and equipment are on the floor.

What happens when I arrive?

Come ten minutes early to meet the coach, see the space, and talk through your goals before the group warms up.
